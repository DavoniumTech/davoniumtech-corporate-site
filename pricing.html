What is included in a quote?

We define scope before development so that the price and expectations are clear on both sides.

Are domain registration and hosting included?

Not by default. Domain registration, hosting, email, premium plugins and third-party subscriptions are listed separately unless a written proposal explicitly includes them.

Can I request a website with a content management system?

Yes. CMS requirements must be specified: who will edit content, which content types are needed, whether user accounts are required, and where the data will be stored. A front-end preview is not a production CMS.

Do online stores include payment processing?

Payment-provider integration can be included when agreed. Provider onboarding, transaction fees, settlement rules and required backend services are separate considerations.

How are delivery time and revisions agreed?

The proposal will list the deliverables, dependencies, revision allowance, estimated schedule, payment milestones and handover responsibilities before work begins.

Can international customers pay in US dollars?

Yes. Select USD as your preferred quotation currency. The currency selector does not convert or publish an unapproved price; the proposal will state the agreed currency and amount.
