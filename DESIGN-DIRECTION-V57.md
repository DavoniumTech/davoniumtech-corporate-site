# V57 Design Direction — Davonium Signal Studio

## Visual language
A deep graphite digital sky with bright fine stars, cyan signal points, restrained violet atmosphere, clean architecture and porcelain light mode. The ambient sky is persistent but low-contrast behind readable content; sections use translucent surfaces rather than opaque blocks.

## Character rules
The full-body supplied DVNM Bot remains the single mascot. The small portrait is cropped directly from that exact artwork. No separate floating character launcher or automatic robot welcome overlay is shown. WhatsApp is the sole floating contact shortcut. Robot images have no artificial shadows.

## Five demo art directions
- DVNM Biz: corporate architecture and service-business journey.
- DVNM Market: premium product still life and commerce discovery.
- DVNM Creative: cinematic design studio and portfolio storytelling.
- DVNM Skills: real learning space and guided course journey.
- DVNM Digital: data-centre environment with the standing DVNM Bot and product-world narrative.

## Printable outreach
Five portrait A4 PDFs plus 200-DPI JPG previews. QR images are generated locally from the canonical demo destinations; generated artwork does not contain the QR code.

The homepage hero includes a Davonium-inspired D-shaped architectural monument and small illuminated signal nodes behind the approved standing DVNM Bot artwork. The official D icon remains unchanged.
