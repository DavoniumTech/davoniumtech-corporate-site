# Davonium Technologies V61 - Signal Constellation

## Design direction
V61 retains the V60 identity and refines it into one continuous, studio-like environment: dark graphite by default, a deliberately light porcelain alternative, a bright and animated starfield across all routes, and independent glass surfaces rather than opaque section slabs.

## Visual and interaction decisions
- Keep the approved D logo, DAVONIUM wordmark, standing DVNM Bot artwork, existing products, routes, documentation, certificates and five demo identities.
- Re-compose the homepage as a confident split hero: large editorial headline and concise company statement on the left, the existing Davonium D portal and standing Bot on the right, and the audience strip below both.
- Keep the page content and section order; remove visual slabs from section wrappers and let each useful card own its translucent surface.
- Add a lightweight canvas starfield with twinkle, slow drift, a small number of brighter cross-shaped star glints, resize support, hidden-tab pause and reduced-motion fallback.
- Give light mode a crisp porcelain environment with darker, legible stars rather than a faded dark-mode inversion. Dark remains the first-visit theme; the visitor can change and retain the choice.
- Use Cormorant Garamond for expressive display headings and Manrope for readable body copy. Existing button design is retained.
- Give each core demo a return-to-Studio arrow while preserving its own routes, content, navigation and identity.
- Add a browser voice volume control and six curated spoken guide topics to DVNM Bot. Speech starts only after a visitor action. Mouth pulses follow browser speech-boundary events, with a restrained fallback rhythm for voices that omit them; chest illumination follows active speech.
- Use a short, optional-motion page transition for internal links. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never delay the first useful paint.
- Service worker cache is versioned to V61.

## Reference interpretation
The uploaded standalone HTML was treated as an implementation/interaction reference for a full-viewport visual layer, clear glass cards and distinct demo experiences. Its generic solar-system artwork, copy and layout were not copied. The accessible A&S Tech reference informed the large left-aligned headline/right-hand visual balance and clear company section hierarchy; the Davonium robot, D monument and content remain original to Davonium.

## Final laptop and background refinement
- The sticky header uses a more transparent, theme-aware glass surface so the starfield remains visible behind navigation without harming menu readability.
- A pair of subtle orbital structures fills the right portion of the desktop hero stage around the existing D monument and standing robot, balancing the left headline without introducing another character or logo.
- Star count, glint frequency and drift were increased while keeping a capped device pixel ratio, 30 fps drawing cadence, hidden-tab pause and reduced-motion behaviour.
