# V59 design direction — Davonium Technologies

V59 closes the visual system around a dark-first, glass-and-starfield digital engineering studio. It keeps the official D mark and standing DVNM Bot art, centres the company hero for laptop widths, removes decorative photography from the three in-development product cards, and gives every route a consistent complete company footer. The fixed bot control uses the actual standing character's head crop, never a drawn baby-robot avatar. Motion remains optional and reduced-motion aware; speech begins only after an explicit visitor action.

The certificate is a self-generated non-official showcase record. Demo pricing, interactions, product sample content and sample projects must not be presented as live customer transactions or accredited qualifications.
