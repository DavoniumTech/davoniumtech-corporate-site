# Content map

- `index.html`: company homepage and current product overview.
- `about.html`: company purpose and principles.
- `products.html`: product descriptions and accurate status labels.
- `services.html`: website, PWA, business systems, prototyping and support services.
- `portfolio.html`: current work and product progress; does not claim unsupported client outcomes.
- `contact.html`: verified contact methods, project brief and email fallback.
- `docs.html`: public documentation hub.
- `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `security.html`, `accessibility.html`: policy and support pages.
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`: discovery metadata/resources.
