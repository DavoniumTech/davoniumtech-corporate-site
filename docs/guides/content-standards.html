Content and editorial standards

Write product copy and technical guides that sound human, accurate, and useful.

Purpose. Write product copy and technical guides that sound human, accurate, and useful. Use this guide as practical information and verify details against the deployed product and any applicable agreement.

Overview and purpose

Every public page should answer a recognisable question and help the reader choose a next step. Use descriptive headings, plain language, relevant examples, and a consistent company voice.

For this area, the team should make the intended behaviour explicit, keep the implementation proportionate to the need, and record any constraint that could affect a user. Where a service relies on a browser feature or external provider, describe that dependency instead of implying universal support.

  • Identify the person, task, and expected result relevant to content and editorial standards.
  • State requirements and limitations in language a non-specialist can follow.
  • Test the common path, an error path, and the recovery route before release.
