Public information for search and AI discovery

How we publish clear, crawlable information about Davonium Technologies for search engines and compatible AI retrieval systems.

What we make public

We publish company information, service descriptions, current product status, public support guidance, and practical guides on our canonical domain. These pages are the authoritative public reference for what Davonium Technologies does and which products are available or still in development.

We do not publish private customer records, passwords, access tokens, internal project notes, or confidential configuration to improve discovery.

Crawler preferences

We allow OAI-SearchBot to access public pages so they may be discovered through ChatGPT search. We separately disallow GPTBot to express that the public site is not offered for that crawler's model-training collection. Other search and retrieval services follow their published crawler names and the rules in our robots.txt.

A crawler policy is a request to compliant bots, not access control. It does not make a public page private or hide information delivered to a visitor's browser.

Machine-readable summaries

We maintain llms.txt as a short index and llms-full.txt as a more complete guide map. They help a compatible retrieval system understand the company and locate relevant public pages; they are optional conventions and may be ignored by individual services.

Search discovery and indexing

We maintain descriptive page titles, useful summaries, consistent canonical addresses, direct links between related guides, and a current XML sitemap. We use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools to review crawl status, submit important URLs, and inspect reported errors.

We cannot promise when a crawler will visit a page, whether a search engine will index it, or whether an AI product will mention or cite it. We prioritise useful, accurate content and fix access problems when evidence shows that a page cannot be reached.

Keep the public record aligned

When a product changes status or a public route changes, we update the relevant page, related guides, sitemap, and machine-readable summaries together. We prefer clear evidence over claims of guaranteed rankings or worldwide visibility.
