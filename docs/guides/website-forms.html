Website forms and contact flows

How we keep forms transparent about what is collected and what happens when a visitor submits a message.

Our current contact flow

Our contact form collects your name, reply email, enquiry topic, and message in the browser, then prepares a mailto: message for your email application.

The form does not send information to a backend or create a server-side support ticket. Your email application needs to open, and you need to send the prepared message.

  • We state the handoff clearly in the interface.
  • We keep the support email visible as a fallback.

Data minimisation

We ask for enough context to understand the enquiry, not for secrets or unnecessary identity details. The message can identify the product, public page, steps taken, and expected outcome.

A public website form is not an appropriate place to submit passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, full card numbers, or private keys.

  • We keep required fields limited to the enquiry need.
  • We avoid collecting a message silently in the background.

Validation and feedback

Client-side checks help a visitor notice missing required fields, but they do not prove that a message reached us. The interface describes the next step instead of pretending that a ticket was created.

If we later add a server-backed contact endpoint, it will require server-side validation, spam control, access restrictions, retention decisions, logging, and updated privacy wording.

  • We test valid input and missing-field behaviour.
  • We confirm the email handoff text matches the actual action.

Maintenance and privacy

We keep contact details consistent across our company pages and update them when ownership or support routing changes.

Our privacy and terms pages remain draft policy text until reviewed against actual production practice and applicable requirements.

  • We check mobile input usability and keyboard focus.
  • We update documentation when the contact workflow changes.

Contact flow boundary

Our current form prepares an email in your mail application; it does not store a submission or create a server-side ticket. We will update this guide if the workflow changes.
