Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about our company, product status, website installation, privacy, search visibility, and support.

What does Davonium Technologies do?

We build digital products for students and everyday users and practical websites and web tools for small businesses. Our website explains our work, services, products, and support routes.

Our products are developed under the Davonium Technologies brand.

  • Ready2Go Learning is a learning and JAMB exam-practice product in development.
  • Marvel Chat’s initial build exists and development continues; Davonium Nexus is in development.

Can I install this website?

On compatible browsers, our website can be installed as a Progressive Web App. The browser and operating system control whether a native installation prompt appears and what it looks like.

The install option is not an Android APK download and cannot be forced by a website.

  • Use the visible Install app link for guidance.
  • On iPhone and iPad, installation generally uses Safari’s Share menu and Add to Home Screen.

Does our website work offline?

Previously visited pages and essential assets may reopen offline after the service worker has installed and the resources have been cached successfully.

A first-time offline visit and links to external product sites require a connection. Browser cleanup can clear cached files.

  • Open important pages while online before relying on them offline.
  • Reconnect to access uncached content or external services.

Can search engines and AI systems discover our site?

We publish crawlable HTML pages, clean internal links, canonical URLs, robots.txt, an XML sitemap, and public AI-discovery information. We permit relevant crawlers in our published robots policy.

Discovery settings improve access signals but do not guarantee indexing, ranking, citations, or inclusion in an AI response.

  • We use Search Console to inspect indexing when available.
  • For a specific question, contact support@davoniumtech.com.
