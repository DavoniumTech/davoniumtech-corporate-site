What this website remembers

What our website stores in the browser for theme preference, installation reminders, and offline access.

Appearance preferences

The website follows your device's light or dark appearance by default. If you explicitly choose a theme, the browser can remember that preference on the same device so the pages remain consistent on your next visit.

The installation reminder

If you dismiss the optional app-install invitation, the website records a small preference so it does not interrupt you on every page. If installation is detected, the invitation can stop appearing in that browser. Clearing browser data may reset these preferences.

Saved pages for offline reopening

The browser may keep selected pages and images so a page you already visited can reopen without a connection. This information stays in the browser's site storage on that device. The browser or device owner can clear it, and storage cleanup or a site update can remove some saved resources.

What these preferences are not

These values do not create a company account, profile, or server-side support ticket. The public company site does not ask visitors to register just to read pages. Separate products can have different features and data practices; review the relevant product information before using them.

Questions or concerns

Our privacy notice and storage information are still under review. Contact our team if you need clarification about a specific interaction.
