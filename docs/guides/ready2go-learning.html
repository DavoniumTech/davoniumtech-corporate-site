Ready2Go Learning product guide

Our product direction for a student-focused learning and JAMB exam-practice experience.

What we are building

We are developing Ready2Go Learning to help students organise study and practise exam-style questions, with an initial focus on JAMB candidates.

Ready2Go Learning is in development. This guide describes direction and principles, not a claim that every planned subject, question pack, or exam mode is live.

  • We organise learning around clear subjects and topics.
  • We aim to make practice sessions understandable on phones and desktop computers.

Learning and question quality

We want questions to be clear, curriculum-aware, and useful for practice. Where a question is presented as an official past question, the source and year need to be represented accurately; generated or authored practice content must not be mislabelled as a verified past question.

We design feedback to help learners understand a mistake rather than simply mark an attempt as a failure.

  • We keep difficulty labels and explanations meaningful.
  • We avoid repeated questions within a session when the session design promises no repetition.

Offline and device experience

Our product direction includes mobile-friendly study and carefully planned offline access. Any offline capability depends on which content has been packaged or cached and how the product handles updates.

Our website has its own PWA installation; that installation is separate from Ready2Go Learning and does not determine the product’s availability.

  • We test session progress and navigation on supported phone and desktop sizes.
  • We explain when a question pack or feature needs a connection.

Current status

Ready2Go Learning remains in development. Access, pricing, supported subjects, question coverage, and release timing may change as the product is completed and tested.

We will update the official product website and this guide as confirmed capabilities are released.

  • We distinguish verified past questions from practice content.
  • We do not promise a release date or exam result.

Where we publish updates

We publish confirmed availability and access details on the official Ready2Go website and update this guide when verified product capabilities change. Until a release is confirmed, we describe Ready2Go Learning as in development rather than promising a launch date or a particular feature.
