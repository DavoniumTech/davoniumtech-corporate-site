Data handling and privacy principles

How we think about personal information, browser storage, access, and clear explanations of what our services do.

Our data-minimisation approach

We start by asking what a feature needs and why. We avoid collecting a field simply because it could be useful later, and we explain the purpose in the relevant product or privacy information.

Our public website does not require an account to read company pages or documentation.

  • We do not ask visitors to send passwords, one-time codes, card PINs, or private keys through public contact routes.
  • We keep support requests focused on the minimum context needed to investigate.

Website storage and contact

Our website uses local browser storage for the theme preference and installation-invitation dismissal, and Cache Storage for selected pages and assets. Our contact form prepares an email in your mail application instead of sending your message to a ticket database. These browser-side behaviours do not mean we operate a website account profile or store a server-side ticket.

  • You can clear site data through your browser settings.
  • The external email application and linked messaging service use their own handling and terms.

Access and security

For any service that stores personal information, we treat access controls, purpose limits, retention, backups, and incident handling as design requirements. Public client-side code cannot hold confidential server credentials.

We describe only the controls we can verify, and we keep product-specific security claims aligned with actual releases.

  • We do not commit secrets to public version control.
  • We use appropriate account roles and remove access when it is no longer needed.

Current policy status

Our privacy, terms, and browser-storage policy pages are draft text and need to be checked against actual production data flows and applicable legal requirements before we treat them as final policies.

We will update those notices if our forms, analytics, accounts, or product storage change materially.

  • We separate technical descriptions from legal promises.
  • For a privacy question, use the published contact route and avoid including sensitive credentials.

How we keep this information current

We review this guide when our storage, analytics, contact handling, authentication, or retention practices change. We only finalise policy wording after checking it against the behaviour of the production service and the review that applies to us.
