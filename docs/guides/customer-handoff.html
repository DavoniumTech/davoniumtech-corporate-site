Customer handoff checklist

How we transfer a website or digital deliverable with its source files, access ownership, and maintenance information clearly recorded.

What we hand over

We describe the delivered pages, functions, content, browser support, integrations, and known limitations in plain language. A clear handoff makes it possible to understand what was completed and what remains outside scope.

We keep the source repository and hosting account ownership explicit rather than relying on access held only by one personal account.

  • We identify the repository, production branch, domain, DNS, and hosting project.
  • We list contact forms, third-party links, external assets, and any recurring services.

Access and ownership

We document which person or organisation owns the domain, repository, hosting account, analytics property, and external services. Access is granted by role and removed when no longer required.

Secrets and account recovery codes do not belong in the repository, public documentation, or browser-delivered HTML and JavaScript.

  • We use account-level invitations where a provider supports them.
  • We record who can publish production changes and how access is recovered.

Deployment and recovery

Our handoff records how the site reaches production, which branch is deployed, where build logs are reviewed, and how to restore a known-good release.

For this corporate website, static assets are deployed through Cloudflare Workers using the checked-in Wrangler configuration.

  • We test direct routes, mobile navigation, contact handoff, and installation behaviour.
  • We retain a known-good commit or branch before a high-impact update.

Acceptance and follow-up

We compare the delivered result against the agreed pages and workflows. We note unresolved tasks and the owner of each follow-up rather than describing them as completed.

Support and maintenance after handoff depend on the agreed scope. We do not imply a monitoring or ticket-management service where none exists.

  • We record accepted deliverables and known limitations.
  • We share the public support channel and the practical steps for reporting a reproducible issue.

Closing a handoff

We close a handoff when the agreed files, ownership, credentials transfer, deployment notes, and unresolved items have been reviewed with the client.
