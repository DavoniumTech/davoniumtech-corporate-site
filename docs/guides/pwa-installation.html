Progressive web app installation

How the Davonium Technologies website can be installed through compatible browsers and what its install prompt can and cannot do.

What installation means

Our company website is a Progressive Web App. On supported browsers, you can add it to your device’s home screen or app list and open it in a more app-like window. This installs our website through your browser and operating system; it is not a native Android APK downloaded from our site.

Our manifest declares the app name, start URL, display mode, and icon variants. Your browser decides whether a native installation prompt is available, and the operating system can apply its own icon mask.

The invitation and browser prompt

We provide an Install app link throughout our website and an optional invitation on entry pages. Where a browser exposes beforeinstallprompt, the Install action invokes that browser-managed prompt. Some browsers do not expose this event; in those cases, we show device-specific manual steps instead of pretending that installation succeeded.

You can dismiss the invitation and keep browsing. We avoid repeatedly interrupting someone who has already dismissed it, and we reduce the chance of showing the invitation again after our website is installed.

Icons and appearance

The manifest provides standard, rounded, and maskable icon assets. A launcher may crop or shape the icon according to its own design rules, so the final appearance can vary by platform.

We keep the central Davonium mark clear and leave the operating system to apply its own final icon mask where appropriate.

  • We test the installed icon after a new manifest or icon release.
  • A browser may keep an older icon until its local app data or installation is refreshed.

Offline behaviour

The service worker caches essential files first and stores pages after successful visits. The installed window can reopen cached resources when offline.

Installation alone does not download every page or make external links work without a connection.

  • We visit important pages online before testing offline use.
  • We treat uncached routes, email handoff, WhatsApp, and product subdomains as network-dependent.

After installation

We verify the app name, launch URL, icon, offline fallback, and theme on actual target devices. Browsers and operating systems may shape installed icons differently.
