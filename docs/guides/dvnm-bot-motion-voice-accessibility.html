DVNM BOT GUIDE

DVNM Bot: movement, voice and appearance

A practical guide to the full-body DVNM character, the dark and light themes, speech controls, responsive layout, and accessibility decisions.

How the character is built

DVNM Bot uses the supplied transparent full-body artwork for its silver-and-blue shell, hands, arms, legs, and illuminated DVNM chest panel. A separately masked head layer is used for small head turns and nods while the body layer keeps the original character intact. The visor overlay draws a clean cyan face display that can animate during speech without relying on large distracting eye shapes.

Speaking and mouth animation

Audio is never started automatically. Press Hear this answer or Hear introduction to use the browser's built-in speech synthesis. Where the browser reports word boundaries, DVNM Bot pulses its mouth for each word. Where boundary events are absent or unreliable, it uses a gentle speaking animation. This is speech-timed animation, not guaranteed phoneme-perfect lip-sync; browser voices, timing, language and audio controls differ across devices.

The DVNM chest image remains readable while a separate light aura pulses around the chest during speech. The robot also makes a small body bob and head movement. Clicks or taps on the page prompt a brief attention reaction and nudge its gaze toward the interaction. This is a lightweight illustration rig, not a physics simulation or a fully modeled 3D avatar.

Choose a device voice

Open Voice and speaking settings and choose Recommended DVNM Voice for automatic selection, or select a voice installed or exposed by the browser. The recommendation prefers a compatible English voice and may favour locally installed voices for responsiveness and offline availability. Voice names are hints rather than a promise about accent or naturalness. Some mobile browsers expose few or no voice choices until the first user interaction or until their voice list has loaded.

Speaking volume and pace can be adjusted. A moderate pace is the default; slow the rate for easier listening or increase it a little for faster delivery. Device media volume still controls the final loudness. Use Stop voice whenever you need to interrupt playback.

Light and dark appearance

The light theme uses calm white surfaces with visible section transitions in mint green, cool gray, warm beige, soft violet, pale indigo, and blush tones. Dark mode returns to the earlier Davonium midnight background with transparent blue-violet glass, restrained cyan and green light, and subtle borders. Cards use reduced blur so the content behind them remains visible instead of looking cloudy. Text and controls retain contrast and clear focus outlines.

Small-screen behaviour

The header menu and the bot panel fit narrow mobile screens, including widths around 320 pixels. The assistant launcher stays above the green WhatsApp contact button; they occupy separate touch areas, so either can be opened independently. The panel uses the viewport height and safe-area insets to avoid clipping against mobile browser controls. Demo pages keep their own page content while sharing the responsive navigation and theme.

Accessibility and privacy

  • Speech requires an explicit visitor action; no automatic audio is played.
  • Written answers remain available if speech is unsupported or fails.
  • Buttons and selectors can be used by keyboard; focus states and Escape-to-close support remain available.
  • Reduced-motion preferences suppress decorative movement.
  • The optional name in the welcome message is used locally for the current visit greeting and is not sent to a server by this feature.
  • Speech uses the browser/device voice engine. The site does not upload the spoken text to an external voice provider.

Troubleshooting

If voice playback does not start, tap Hear again after the page finishes loading, confirm the device's media volume, try Recommended DVNM Voice, and use Stop voice if playback appears stuck. After an update, refresh the page once; installed PWAs may retain older cached assets until the service worker activates. If a layout issue remains, test the homepage and one demo page at 320px, 360px, 390px, tablet and desktop widths.
