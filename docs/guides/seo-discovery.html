How we help people find our public information

How we make our public pages understandable and crawlable for search engines and compatible AI retrieval systems.

Make important information easy to reach

We give our company pages and public guides their own stable addresses and connect related topics through ordinary links. A visitor should be able to open a product guide, support page, or service description directly rather than needing to begin at the homepage.

Our preferred public address is https://www.davoniumtech.com/. We keep internal links, canonical addresses, social previews, and sitemap entries aligned with that domain so the same page is not unintentionally promoted under competing URL forms.

Write for the person searching

We use page titles and summaries that describe what the reader will find. Product pages explain the audience, purpose, and current development status. Documentation titles reflect the question being answered rather than repeating a generic label across every guide.

Keep the sitemap useful

Our XML sitemap lists the preferred addresses for our public company and documentation pages. We omit error pages, offline fallback pages, and draft policy pages that we have marked as not ready for search. We reference the sitemap in robots.txt and submit it through Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

Read indexing reports correctly

A submitted URL or sitemap is a discovery signal, not confirmation that the page is indexed. We review the reported response, selected canonical, crawl permissions, content quality, and any errors shown by the search service. We avoid repeatedly submitting the same unchanged URL in the hope of moving it ahead in a queue.

Keep improving the pages themselves

Discovery is only useful if the page answers the visitor's question. We keep public guides specific, update product status honestly, remove broken links, and give visitors a clear route to related information or support. No checklist can guarantee a search position or inclusion in an AI-generated answer.
