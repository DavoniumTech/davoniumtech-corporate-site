Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Applies to the Davonium Technologies company website.

Scope matters. This policy describes the company website at davoniumtech.com. Separate products, including Ready2Go Learning and Marvel Chat, may process information under their own product-specific notices and technical settings.

1. Information this website handles

This is a static information website. It does not provide a visitor account or an on-site account registration form. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, the information you send—such as your name, contact details, project description and message—is handled by the communication provider and our team to respond to your enquiry.

Like other websites, the hosting and network providers may process technical request information such as IP address, requested URL, browser details, timestamps and security events in order to deliver, protect and troubleshoot the site.

2. Preferences stored on your device

The site may store your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage and use a service worker to cache site files for faster loading and offline access. These features are intended to remember a display preference and support the installable website; they are not used here to create an advertising profile.

3. How information is used

  • Respond to messages and project enquiries.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the website.
  • Understand and investigate technical problems or abuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations where they apply.

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, private keys or highly sensitive personal information in a general enquiry.

4. Service providers and external links

The website is planned to be hosted through Cloudflare Pages and may use Cloudflare network security and caching services. Links to products, email, telephone, WhatsApp and other websites lead to third-party services with their own privacy practices. When you follow those links, their policies apply to the information they collect.

5. Retention and security

Enquiries may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, follow up, keep business records or meet applicable legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the website and the information involved, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children and student products

The company website is not intended to collect student records. Some Davonium products may be used by students, including younger users, and may have separate account, parental-consideration, retention and consent requirements. Read the relevant product's own privacy information before creating an account or submitting data.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit an enquiry, clear local website data from your browser, or contact us to ask about personal information you have previously provided. We may need to verify a request before acting on it and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website and company services change. The effective date above will be changed when a substantive revision is published.

Email: support@davoniumtech.com · Alternative: davoniumtechnologies@gmail.com.

This policy is a practical starting point and should be reviewed for compliance with the laws applicable to the company and its actual data practices before relying on it as final legal advice.
