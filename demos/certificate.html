What this document records

We use this showcase record to document the seven concept websites included in our Demo Studio: DVNM Biz, DVNM Growth, DVNM Market, DVNM Creative, DVNM Skills, DVNM Digital, and DVNM Connect. Each QR code points to one specific demonstration page on the Davonium Technologies domain.

What it does not certify

This is a non-official portfolio and technology showcase record. It is not a government document, accredited qualification, professional licence, university certificate, employment reference, or independent audit. It does not certify any visitor's skills or claim that a prototype is a commissioned customer delivery.

How to use the QR codes

  1. Open the PDF on a laptop or another screen.
  2. Scan the chosen demo's QR code with a phone camera.
  3. Review the concept page and its prototype limitations before discussing a production website.

Our commitment

We aim to be clear about ownership, product status, demo-only interactions, and the work needed before a prototype becomes a production service. Sample prices, products, venues, events, and case studies are illustrative until a real project is scoped and approved.
