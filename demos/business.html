A clear customer journey

Help a customer understand the next step.

This sample illustrates how a service business can explain its work before a visitor makes contact. A real business would replace the example content with verified details.

01

Understand the service

Explain what the business does, who it helps, what a customer should prepare, and which requests fall outside the service.

02

Check the details

Give a clear service area, confirmed opening hours, accessibility information, expected response route, and current terms.

03

Make an enquiry

Use a visible contact route and set expectations about what happens next, without collecting sensitive information unnecessarily.

Can this design work for organisations of different sizes?

Yes. The layout can be scaled for independent professionals, startups, established companies, multi-location organisations, and larger teams. Real content and requirements must be confirmed before launch.
