Make the work tell the right story.

A production portfolio can include approved images, accessible case studies, clear project roles, real outcomes where verified, and a useful enquiry path.

Sample portfolio brief

Portfolio storytelling

Show the process, role, and context.

A professional portfolio should help visitors understand contribution and working approach, not simply display a collection of attractive images.

01

Define the brief

Describe the problem, audience, constraints, and success criteria that shaped the work.

02

Explain your contribution

Separate your individual responsibilities from team efforts and clearly label sample, practice, or commissioned work.

03

Share verified outcomes

Use approved screenshots and factual results. Do not invent a client, testimonial, performance metric, or project relationship.
