SECURITY

Report a security concern.

We welcome responsible, private reports that help protect users and improve our products.

How to report

If you believe you have found a security issue in a Davonium Technologies website or product, please email the details to support@davoniumtech.com with “Security report” in the subject. If the support mailbox has not yet been activated, use davoniumtechnologies@gmail.com.

What to include

  • The product and URL affected.
  • A clear description of the issue and its possible impact.
  • Minimal steps to reproduce it, with screenshots where helpful.
  • Your preferred contact method for clarification.

Responsible testing

Do not access other users' data, disrupt service, persist in accounts or systems you do not own, publish personal data, or perform destructive testing. Stop if you unexpectedly encounter sensitive information and report the issue privately.

Response expectations

We will review reports as time and resources permit. This page does not promise a response deadline, a bounty or a specific outcome. Please allow time for investigation before publicly disclosing a suspected vulnerability.

Scope

Only test systems you own or have explicit permission to test. Reports about third-party services should be sent to their owners.
