Cookies & Browser Storage

This is our draft cookies and browser-storage notice. We are checking it against the website’s actual production behaviour before final adoption.

Review status. We are reviewing this notice against the website’s actual storage behaviour and applicable requirements.

Browser preferences

Our website uses browser storage for interface preferences, such as your explicit light or dark theme choice and whether you have dismissed the optional installation invitation. By default, the theme follows the device setting.

Offline caching

Our offline cache keeps selected public pages and resources so the website can reopen some previously visited routes when a connection is unavailable. Cached pages are not a backup, and online actions or external links do not automatically work offline.

Third-party tools

We do not intentionally include advertising or remote analytics scripts in this website release. Our hosting provider and linked services may have their own technical practices. We are checking the live production configuration before finalising this inventory.

Managing storage

You can clear our site data through your browser settings. Doing so may reset your theme preference, remove offline copies, and allow the installation invitation to appear again. Browsers and private sessions handle storage differently.

Future changes

If we add analytics, embedded media, advertising, or other third-party scripts, we will review this notice and any consent mechanism against the laws that apply to our service and its visitors.

For questions about this draft notice, email us at support@davoniumtech.com.
