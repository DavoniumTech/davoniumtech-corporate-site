Cookies & Browser Storage

This page is a draft policy for the Davonium Technologies company website and must be checked against actual production practices before final adoption.

Review status. This policy page is a working draft. Confirm that it matches the site’s actual data handling, services, and legal obligations before treating it as final.
Draft for review. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice. Verify the deployed setup and seek qualified legal review before treating it as final.

Browser preferences

The website may use browser storage to remember interface preferences such as light or dark theme and whether the optional first-visit installation invitation has already been dismissed.

Service worker caching

A service worker can cache selected public files and pages so a previously visited website may still open when a connection is unavailable. Cached pages are not a substitute for a backup, and all online workflows do not automatically work offline.

Third-party tools

The package does not intentionally add advertising or remote analytics scripts by default. Hosting providers and linked services may have their own technical practices. Confirm the live production configuration before treating this as a final inventory.

Managing storage

Visitors can clear site data through browser settings. This may reset theme preferences, remove offline copies, and cause the install invitation to appear again. Some browsers and private sessions handle storage differently.

Future changes

If analytics, embedded media, advertising, or other third-party scripts are added, review whether this notice and any consent mechanism need to change under the law applicable to the service and its visitors.
