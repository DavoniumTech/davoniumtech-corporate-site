Privacy Notice

This is our draft privacy notice. We are checking it against the website’s actual production practices before adopting a final policy.

Review status. We are reviewing this notice against our actual data handling, services, and applicable legal obligations.

Who is responsible

We operate this public company website as Davonium Technologies. For questions about this notice, contact support@davoniumtech.com. This draft covers our website only; our separate products and third-party services may have their own privacy notices.

Information you choose to send

When you contact us by email, phone, or WhatsApp, the channel you choose receives the information you send. This may include your reply address, project details, attachments, or other material you provide voluntarily.

Website and browser information

Our hosting provider may process technical request information and operational logs to deliver and protect the website. We are verifying the live provider settings, exact log data, retention period, and any additional tools before finalising this notice.

Contact form behaviour

Our static contact form prepares a message in your configured email application. It does not send the form to a company database or create a support ticket. You review and send the message from your email application.

Purpose, access, and retention

When we receive an enquiry, we use the supplied details to understand and answer the request and to maintain appropriate business records. We limit access to people who need the information for those purposes. We are still confirming the precise retention and deletion schedule for our production processes.

Security and choices

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, while recognising that no website can promise absolute security. You can manage browser data through your device settings. Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or payment-card security details in ordinary messages.

Legal review

The rights and obligations that apply to us depend on the service, the data involved, and the relevant jurisdiction. We are reviewing this draft against our actual operations and qualified legal advice; it is not legal advice or a claim of complete compliance.

Contact

For questions about this draft notice, email us at support@davoniumtech.com.
