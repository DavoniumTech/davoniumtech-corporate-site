Privacy Notice

This page is a draft policy for the Davonium Technologies company website and must be checked against actual production practices before final adoption.

Draft for review. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice. Verify the deployed setup and seek qualified legal review before treating it as final.

Who is responsible

Davonium Technologies operates this company website. For questions about this notice, use support@davoniumtech.com. This draft applies to the public corporate website described here; it should not automatically be treated as the final notice for separate products or third-party services.

Information you choose to send

If you contact the team by email, phone, or WhatsApp, the selected channel receives the information you choose to include. Messages may contain your reply address, project details, attachments, or other material you provide voluntarily.

Website and browser information

The hosting provider may process technical request information and operational logs to serve and protect the website. The live provider settings, log details, retention period, and any additional tools must be confirmed before this statement is final.

Contact form behaviour

The static contact form prepares a message for the visitor’s configured email application. It does not itself send a request to a company database or create a ticket. The visitor must review and send the message from their email application.

Purpose, access, and retention

Information supplied in an enquiry should be used to understand and answer the request and maintain appropriate business records. Access should be limited to people who need it for those purposes, and retention and deletion practices should be defined for the real service.

Security and choices

Use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but do not promise absolute security. Visitors can manage browser data through their device settings. Never send passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or payment-card security details in ordinary messages.

Legal review

Rights and obligations depend on the service, the type of data, users, and applicable jurisdictions. This is a draft for operational and qualified legal review, not legal advice or a claim of full compliance.

Contact

For questions, email support@davoniumtech.com.
