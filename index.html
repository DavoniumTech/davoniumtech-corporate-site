Meet your digital guide

Say hello to DVNM Bot.

Meet DVNM Bot, our animated digital guide. Ask about our products, services, documentation, or seven demo websites, then follow the links to explore.

  • Choose a question; get a clear written answer and useful link.
  • Press “Hear the answer” to use your device’s natural speech voice.
  • Watch the character blink, turn and nod its head, glow at the DVNM chest, and move its mouth while speech plays.
  • Use keyboard-friendly controls with no automatic audio.
How DVNM Bot works

Voice uses the browser’s built-in speech synthesis, so available voices vary by device. Mouth movement follows available word-boundary timing where supported; browser and device timing can vary, so it is not phoneme-perfect lip-sync.

How we think

Technology should make the next step clearer.

The best experience is not necessarily the one with the most features. It helps people get the job done, understand what is happening, and trust what comes next.
