Meet your digital guide

Say hello to DVNM Bot.

A glowing, animated visitor guide for Davonium Technologies, built to welcome guests and explain our products, services, documentation, and sample demonstrations. DVNM Bot welcomes visitors, answers common questions, and points people to our products, services, demo websites, documentation, and contact details.

  • Choose a question; get a clear written answer and useful link.
  • Press “Hear the answer” to use your device’s natural speech voice.
  • Watch the character blink, wave, float, and animate its mouth while speech plays.
  • Use keyboard-friendly controls with no automatic audio.
How DVNM Bot works

Voice uses the browser’s built-in speech synthesis, so available voices vary by device. Mouth movement follows available word-boundary timing where supported; browser and device timing can vary, so it is not phoneme-perfect lip-sync.
