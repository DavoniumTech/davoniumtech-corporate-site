OUR PURPOSE

Make useful technology feel possible.

We believe technology earns its place when it solves a real problem, respects the people who use it and can be improved over time. Our work spans our own digital products, websites, web applications and practical business tools.

We are building the company product by product. That means being honest about what is live, what is being tested and what remains an idea. We value progress, but not at the expense of clarity, privacy or reliability.

WHAT GUIDES US

Principles we can actually use.

These are not claims that the work is perfect. They are standards to return to when deciding what to build and how to improve it.

✦

Useful over flashy

Visual quality matters, but every feature should make the experience clearer or more valuable.

⌘

Responsible foundations

We pay attention to data, access controls, performance, maintainability and clear limitations.

↗

Progress with honesty

We label prototypes and work in progress accurately and improve them based on real tests.
