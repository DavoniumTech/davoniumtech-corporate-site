Davonium Technologies is an independent technology company building software, applications, digital products, websites, web tools, and tailored technology services for people and organisations worldwide. Our work starts with the people who will use the experience and the problem they need to solve.

We describe our products honestly and label each initiative clearly, so you can distinguish released features from work still in progress.

How we work

  • Start with the audience, constraints, and task before choosing a solution.
  • Keep the first-release scope explicit and separate future ideas.
  • Build for mobile screens, slower connections, keyboard access, and recoverable errors.
  • Make product status and limitations clear.
  • Include documentation, testing, and handoff in the delivery process.
