# Davonium Technologies — V56 Design Direction

## Creative thesis: Signal Architecture

Davonium should read as a digital engineering studio: cinematic enough to be remembered, useful enough to be trusted. The visual signature is a monumental architectural portal inspired by connected systems, illuminated by restrained cyan signal light, with the existing DVNM Bot character as a friendly guide. This is Davonium's own geometry and palette; Lusion is a source of craft principles, not a layout or asset to copy.

## Page architecture

1. **Header:** approved brand mark, compact Work / Services / Products / Studio / About navigation, theme control and one project CTA. Documentation, Support and Install remain discoverable through the site and footer rather than competing for the primary nav.
2. **Hero:** “Digital products, engineered for impact.” Left-aligned copy, two actions (Explore our work, Start a project), and a composed portal scene with the approved transparent robot artwork.
3. **Audience cues:** individuals/founders, growing teams, enterprises and organisations.
4. **Demo Studio:** five original Davonium experiences, each using its own visual artwork and route. Do not style them as five colour variants of a single template.
5. **DVNM Bot:** compact visitor guide with curated answers and optional speech; voice never auto-plays.
6. **Products:** Ready2Go Learning, Marvel Chat and Davonium Nexus with honest development statuses.
7. **Services:** websites/web apps; software and business systems; Android/mobile apps; reporting/integrations; PWAs; SEO/documentation.
8. **Delivery process:** Discover; Design; Build & test; Release & support.
9. **PWA, QR, company principles and enquiry CTA:** keep useful existing features and explain them clearly.
10. **Footer:** company/contact, products, demos, docs, install, support and legal navigation.

## Visual system

- Dark: deep graphite/near-black, high contrast, cyan signal light, satin metal, restrained violet depth.
- Light: porcelain/pale neutral, crisp charcoal typography, daylight-like shadows and the same cyan signal accent.
- Typography: Inter already present in the project; use a disciplined hierarchy and readable line lengths.
- Surfaces: fewer, better cards; calm border treatment and deliberate spacing.
- Motion: short, purposeful transitions with a settled resting state; no endless loading veil, mandatory audio or random particle storms.
- Robot: preserve the approved white/silver DVNM Bot identity. The homepage uses the transparent image artwork. The optional Three.js portal is an actual WebGL architectural layer, not a claim that the character image itself is a live model.

## What to keep

Approved logo/icon and product icons; the five demo routes and their original artwork; product descriptions/status; docs and legal guides; PWA/install and offline support; contact and WhatsApp paths; certificate examples with their demo disclaimer; shared DVNM Bot identity; reduced-motion and WebGL fallback pathways.

## What to remove or avoid

Generic solar-system wallpaper as a universal brand identity; placeholder demo windows when original artwork exists; fake FPS/quality claims; fake progress indicators; duplicate assistant launchers; nonfunctional demo/search controls; unsupported testimonials, client logos, metrics, awards or product launch claims; heavy 3D behind long documentation pages.

## What to add

A recognisable architectural portal, clearer audience and service journeys, actual artwork in the five demo previews, a four-step delivery story, a better responsive card composition, and optional 3D that respects device capability, visibility and reduced-motion preference.
